Why Banggai Wonderland?

Banggai Wonderland is created to introduce the hidden natural beauty of Luwuk, Banggai and Banggai Islands to travelers from around the world.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Banggai suitable for foreign travelers? +

Yes, while more remote than Bali, it offers an untouched experience. Basic English is spoken by guides, and our packages make navigation easy.

How do I travel from Luwuk to Salakan? +

We use the daily public ferry or speedboats, taking approximately 2-3 hours depending on the vessel and weather conditions.

Can I customize the trip? +

Absolutely. Let us know your preferred destinations in the booking form under "Special Requests".
